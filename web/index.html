How it works

Thousands of filings a day.
Three things you need to know.

01

Reads every Form 4

When a CEO, CFO, director or 10% owner buys or sells their own company's stock, they must report it to the SEC within two business days. Keyhole reads those filings for you.

02

Splits routine from real

Most insider selling is pre-scheduled under a 10b5-1 plan, months ahead. That is routine. A large sale without a plan, or a CEO buying with their own cash, is not.

03

Tells your agent

Ask Claude "what are insiders doing in my stocks?" and get a plain answer with every number linked to the original filing. Read-only: it can never place an order.

The engine

What happens between the SEC
and your answer.

No AI guessing, no paid data feed. Keyhole is a small open-source program that reads the official filings and does five things, in this order.

  1. 01

    Fetch

    Pulls Form 4 filings straight from SEC EDGAR: the company's own filing history, or the live feed of new filings across the whole US market.

    Free official data · no API key · max 8 requests/s · cached on disk
  2. 02

    Parse

    Reads each filing: who traded (CEO, CFO, director, 10% owner, or a fund), the transaction code, shares, price, and how many shares they hold after.

    P = bought in the market · S = sold in the market · A, M, F, G = grants, option exercises, tax, gifts (not signals)
  3. 03

    Plan check

    Looks for a 10b5-1 plan: the checkbox on the filing, or a footnote saying the trade was set up months in advance. Those trades are routine.

    Pre-scheduled = routine · no plan = the insider chose this moment
  4. 04

    Score

    Every open-market buy gets a 0 to 100 score from who bought, how much, how much it grew their stake, and whether other insiders bought too.

    70+ strong · 45+ notable · below 45 minor
  5. 05

    Read

    Sums it up in plain words per company: net buying, routine selling, or unplanned selling. Every trade links back to the original SEC filing.

    Read-only · it can never place an order

For your AI agent

Five tools. One question away.

insider_activity

Everything insiders did in one company, with a plain-words read.

latest_insider_buys

The newest open-market buys across the US market, ranked 0 to 100.

latest_insider_sales

The biggest new sales, flagged when they were not pre-scheduled.

check_holdings

Your stocks, the ones that need attention first. Reads your Ballast portfolio too.

explain_codes

What every Form 4 code means, and which ones are real signals.
